# Touchline — developer notes

Single-file HTML game (`dist/touchline.html` for the artifact, `dist/touchline-standalone.html` to open locally). Source in `src/`, built by `python3 build.py`. No framework or bundler: the build concatenates plain JS so the engine can also run headless in Node (`test/`).

## Version 18.4 (sideline camera, zoom levels, figure polish, ball touches; 3-hour update)
Plan: `ANIMATION_PLAN.md` "v18.4 execution plan". Built as three units (camera and figures by agents in worktrees, touches in the main session), each reviewed adversarially by two independent reviewers and fixed before the merge.
- **Sideline camera** `src/v17/74_sideline.js` (+ css, `test/sideline.js`): `PREFS.view='sideline'` (Broadcast · Sideline · Tactical in the live bar and the goal-replay card, built by `v184ViewSeg`; V cycles the three). In 3-D a wrapper round `camPose` stands the eye on the near touchline (z 70.7, inside the near rail at 71.6) at 2.0 m: it slides along the line after the play through a critically damped spring (x 18..87, at most 4.5 m/s, no bob); the head follows a smoothed ball point with a small lead (turn rate capped at 69 deg/s, a clamp keeps the ball in the inner ~60 % of the frame), aims higher for far play and tilts down on near-square and tall (phone) frames; anything within 3.6 m makes the eye step back toward the rail and along the line. Broadcast to sideline is a 170 ms fade cut; restarts, half time and leaving Tactical cut. Reduced motion holds a framing and cuts when the ball nears the edge. The director (66) skips its kick-off, corner and penalty presets and the far-side lift in sideline view; replays and the celebration close-up still play; the walkout keeps its own preset. In 2-D, sideline is the broadcast drawing framed tighter (camera width x0.72); `proj` is unchanged.
- **Zoom levels** (same file): `PREFS.zoom` wide / normal / close / tight (default normal). Broadcast: factor 1.3 / 1 / 0.78 / 0.6 on `cam.tw` in `zoomTo` and `resize` through `Stage.v184K`, only on stages built with `{uzoom:true}` (live match, goal-replay card), so scripted framings keep their relative zoom and changes ease in. Sideline: vertical lens 46 / 34 / 26 / 19 degrees (wider on narrow frames), eased. Tactical ignores zoom (buttons `aria-disabled`, label "Fit"). Controls `v184ZoomCtl` (− / level / +), keys + = and − (Ctrl/Cmd/Alt left to the browser), mouse wheel over the match canvas or replay stage (one level per notch, 240 ms cooldown on event time, `preventDefault` only when the level moves). Keyboard hint updated; the phone bar wraps into four rows.
- **Figures** `src/v17/63_p3d_figures.js` (+ `test/fig3d_sheet.js` close/perf modes): ball-joint caps (`v18FCap`) on thigh/shin, upper arm/forearm, shoulder and ankle so bent knees and elbows stay round; outlines about 1.2 px on screen and pushed 3.5 cm back from the camera (`uOlZ`) so the seams between parts of one body disappear; a near LOD at tier A only (limbs one detail level up for figures over 150 CSS px, +/-15 px hysteresis); one-band faces (normals lean forward), hair thinning into the scalp, fuller boot instep, rounder toe, smaller nose. Tier A wide frame: triangles 837k -> 866k; close sideline frame 101 draw calls / 576k triangles (v18.3: 87 / 543k); tiers B and C unchanged.
- **Ball touches** `src/v17/75_touches.js` (+ `test/touches.js`, `test/touches_sheet.js`); `ui_stage.js` only factors the dribble lead into `Stage.prototype.dribLead` (same behaviour). Dribble touches are counted on the stride phase `p.ph` (a contact at 90 % of each foot's cycle; every 1st / 2nd / 3rd contact is a touch when walking / jogging / sprinting, shorter and closer with a defender within 4 m, sooner after a sharp turn but never inside the foot's reach window); after a touch the ball runs ahead on a constant-deceleration profile and is met again; the lead shrinks at the end of a run and comes back about as fast as he moves (no roll-back). In `poseOf` the touching foot meets the back of the ball around each touch, a received pass is met by one foot chosen once per reception (ball side, else the swinging foot) and cushioned, and strikes are retimed so contact happens at release (the stage releases at kickT+kickW), with the standing foot planted on a fixed spot of turf beside the ball. All touch offsets are rate-limited (`v184Limit`) so no foot target jumps. Measured in the lab: touch foot 0.13-0.23 m from the ball at every touch; kicking foot 0.05-0.18 m from the ball at release (was 0.54-0.65 m); no foot pops; carried ball never rolls back; switch `V184T.on`.
- **Integration fixes** (found by a read-only cross-unit review of the merge): in sideline view the walkout skips its crane (which flies to the high broadcast shot) and fades straight to the touchline camera (a director `pick` wrapper at the end of 74); the goal-replay card snaps the camera at each `clipStart` instead of walking the sideline eye across the pitch; in 3-D the touch and strike contacts use the 3-D figure scale (0.92 vs 1.06 x hgt/70), the 3-D ball radius and the boot length (`v184Off`), so the boot meets the ball instead of sinking into it.
- **Gate results on the merged build** (this 4-CPU machine, software GL, several suites in parallel): touches, sync (no loose/far balls, no bad launches or feeds), sideline (all checks), stage2, stage3d_smoke, anim, punt, previews3d, polish, kickoff, replay, v8ui, figures, goalcap pass; touches and sideline pass again after the integration fixes; walkout3d failed one load-bound check ('the camera stands near the called player', 23.4 m) with five browsers running and passed alone. stage3d fails two wall-clock checks ('lab pens: the director picks the penalty camera', 'live 2x: replay plays and a tap skips it'); the v18.3 build (ccc59ed dist, same test file) fails the same two plus a third under the same load, so they are not caused by v18.4. A quieter re-run of stage3d on v18.4 is recorded in the next commit. The full `run_all.sh` was not run (about 45 suites; the v18.4-relevant ones above were). Screenshots: `test/plan/v184_shots.js` -> `test/shots/v184/final/` (desktop night and titanium, phone night and gold; broadcast at four zooms, sideline at four zooms, tactical), all looked at, no page errors.
- **Not verified**: a real GPU (headless software GL runs at 0.6-5 fps, so the feel of the camera springs and zoom easing at 60 fps is unseen); Safari/Firefox, touch devices (no pinch zoom), screen readers; reduced motion in sideline view (read, not run); the walkout-to-sideline handover by eye; sideline in the 3-D replay card; shootouts, penalties and far-side corners in sideline view (filmed from up to 37-70 m, ball small unless zoomed in). ## Version 18.3 (tackles and steals, punt boot; 23-minute update)
- `src/v17/72_tackles.js`: `Stage.prototype.tackle` replaced. Slides on about 10 % of challenges, only from 1.2-3 m and from the side or behind-side, with a new pose (run-in, drop onto the hip, lead leg long and low, trailing leg tucked, hand on the turf, pop-up) carried by the knee-slide glide (no x/y jumps); a missed slide gets up and chases again. Other wins are a standing poke (P.act 'poke'), a shoulder-to-shoulder nudge and touch, or an interception with a stretched leg; the tackler always runs in with chase steering and the ball travels from the carrier's foot over a short launch. Out of reach when time runs out: a heavy touch rolls the ball to him. `fetch` no longer moves an unreachable fetcher; the ball rolls to him. Measured (2-D lab and a live 4x match): every change of possession to the other side had the winner 0.49-0.62 m from the ball; no player moved more than 1.5 m per 120 Hz step.
- `src/v17/73_touchup.js`: during a punt the pose carries `P.bootT` (toe pitch per foot): toe down on the backswing, laces through the ball at contact, toe up-forward in the follow-through. One-expression hooks read it in the 2-D boot angle (`drawFigure`) and the 3-D boot tilt (`63_p3d_figures.js`). The keeper's facing already matches the punt target.
- Not verified: the new tackles and the punt boot in 3-D by eye (same poses, not rendered); a 4x live run showed 44 ball steps of 1.6-2.1 m that look like one long kick, not confirmed; the full suite was not re-run (anim, punt, kickoff, sync, stage3d_smoke pass).

## Version 8 (celebrations, The Courier, TouchlineTV, Senior Night, broadcast engine overhaul)
- **Ball physics** (`ui_stage.js`): passes are aimed at where the receiver will be and home in over the last 70% of the flight; ground passes decelerate like a rolling ball, lofted balls follow a parabola with drag. After a shot crosses the line (or misses) the flight hands its exit velocity to a free-ball integrator (`startFree`/`stepFree`): gravity, bounces (restitution 0.5), rolling friction, ball-stop fences behind the goals and boards along the touchlines. `ball.z` is the underside of the ball; it is drawn and collided with radius `BALL_R` (0.24 m).
- **Net physics**: the goal is a box of four planes (two side nets, back net, roof). A ball that entered through the mouth is held inside by a spring-damper mesh; a ball outside bounces off the outer mesh (side netting, roof). The drawn net deforms by exactly the penetration at the contact point (`netD`), then wobbles back, so the ball can never appear to pass through. Goals cross the line inside the frame (whole ball under the bar and inside the posts); misses cross clear of the frame.
- **Players**: two-bone IK legs and arms (`ik2`), stride cadence tied to speed, lean from acceleration/braking, smooth turns through the camera (`fs`), breathing idle. Kicks have a backswing → contact → follow-through, and the ball is released at contact (`launch` waits out the backswing; `windOf`). Dead balls get a run-up, turns with the ball are drag-backs, dribbling knocks the ball a yard ahead every couple of strides, tackles poke the ball loose to the tackler, keepers gather and throw. Players can never step through a goal's netting (`netBlock`).
- **Signature celebrations** (`CELEBS` in `engine.js`): ten moves unlocked by real milestones (career goals/assists/games, a season goal tally, captaincy, rivalry goal, postseason goal, hat trick). `celebFor` picks the equipped one or an unlocked one at random (Auto). Feats are recorded in `finishMatch`; `migrate` (v8) rebuilds them from the archive. The Stage plays the celebration after every goal (live, replays, TouchlineTV, menu showcase), teammates mob the scorer, and the broadcast captions its name. Player profile → Celebration tab to preview and equip.
- **The Courier** (Newsroom): one edition per round (`courierIssues`), lead story written from the real log (headline, deck, goal-by-goal paragraph with game state, possession/shots, coach quote), a press photo rendered by the broadcast engine from the recorded goal (`shootPhoto`), scoreboard, player of the week, standings, Golden Boot race and notebook. Back issues are browsable.
- **TouchlineTV**: Top 5 plays per issue (`topPlays`: type difficulty, late/decisive goals, postseason, rivalry, build-up length), replayed back-to-back on the broadcast stage with a lower third.
- **Senior Night**: the last regular-season home Varsity game (`isSeniorNight`). Pregame ceremony (framed jersey, portrait, career totals, family with flowers) before any walkout; skippable; Settings → Senior Night ceremony. Archived in `C.seniorNights` and shown in the Hall of Fame.
- **UI**: compare finder (search, team and level filter, sort, full player cards, head-to-head bars); standings and brackets use badge-backed mascot logos; bigger bracket cards; menu with a Continue hero (Enter), live scorebug for the showcase match, news ticker, How to play and What's new; press box on the Office. Fixed a CSS clash that made gold-tier (82+) player cards' text transparent.
- **Tests**: `test/v8.js` (engine) and `test/v8ui.js` (Chromium). `test/sync.js` now also reports interceptions/pokes separately from far steals and treats a placed dead ball as owned.

## Version 5 (match logic, awards, balance)
- Engine: new `break` (Breakaway) chance type, more likely with a high-tempo or counter-attacking side; every open-play, long-range, solo and cross chance now rolls how much space the shooter had (`SPACE_X`: open ×1.6, normal ×1, tight ×0.45 to its xG) and records it as `ev.sp`. Overall scoring was re-levelled (shot rate 0.288, open-play xG .104); calibration still holds (predicted 44/54/65/75/86/95% → observed 48/53/65/73/85/92%).
- Build-ups (`buildSeq`, cosmetic stream) always move forward: deeper players start the move in rising bands, then the assister, then the scorer.
- Trait/opportunity weights softened (`TRAIT`, opp divisors, hot-hand cap 1.2): a 10 in Goal scoring takes a striker from 0.38 to ~0.8 goals a game (about 40–45% of his team's goals) instead of 65%; teammates keep scoring.
- Stage logic: players route round goal frames (`detour`): in and out through the mouth, round the post outside, never through side or back netting (`test/sync.js` counts crossings: 0). Over-the-bar shots are above the bar at the line; misses settle beside the goal. Shots are taken from ≤ ~30 m; the shooter must reach his spot first. Defenders are staged to match `ev.sp` (open = beaten and trailing, tight = one blocking, one tight). Turnovers: the nearest opponent wins it; interceptions only when he is in the passing lane; tackles need real contact (no teleport) and show a slide or a challenge, the victim stumbles; fouls knock the player down and he gets up. Attackers near goal don't pass 18 m+ backwards.
- Classic black-and-white ball: 12 pentagons on icosahedron vertices, tumbling with spin (canvas), and `ballSVG()` for UI icons.
- Full time: the referee takes the ball, winners huddle and celebrate, losers trudge, a draw ends in a handshake line; a full-time card shows the score (user left), scorers and player of the match.
- Summaries (results modal, result tiles, match report header/summary/comparison) put the user's school on the left.
- Awards page: MVP hero, award cards with portraits and the stats behind them (`awardStats`), All-League rows with key numbers; Awards Night cards show the same stat chips.

## Version 4 (flow, pause, kickoffs, squad hub)
- Stage clock: every wait runs on the Stage's own clock (`wait()` is a deadline waiter, not `setTimeout`), so `stage.paused` freezes the ball, players, timers, captions and the match minute instantly. Pause shows an overlay; resume continues the same animation (no loop restart unless the loop was idle). `test/pause.js` checks positions, clock and feed are identical across a 3 s pause.
- Passing sanity (`Stage.sensible`, `relayFor`): engine touches are cosmetic, so implausible ones are rewritten: no back-passes to the keeper unless the passer is within ~32 m of his own goal; opposing keepers never leave the box to win the ball; the nearest defender, not a distant one, makes the challenge; passes over ~34 m go through a teammate in between; about half of turnovers are interceptions rather than chases.
- Kickoffs (`kickoffSpots`, `kickoffShape`, `tapOff`): both teams in their own half in a compressed version of their formation, the defending team outside the centre circle, kicker and partner at the spot; play starts only when everyone is in place, with a tap to the partner. After a goal the keeper retrieves the ball, plays it short to a defender, who finds the kicker while everyone jogs back. `test/kickoff.js` verifies the shape.
- Movement: steering with acceleration and arrival easing, jog speed when holding shape, separation so players don't overlap, facing hysteresis (players face the ball when standing). Figures lean into runs, keepers crouch in a set position, and outlines keep them readable.
- Quiet minutes keep to the chosen speed (extra touches are dropped once 55% of the minute's time is used); goals, shots and cards always play in full.
- Odds are capped at -1000 / +1000 (`ODDS_CAP`).
- Squad hub: team header with XI rating ring and unit bars, formation chips with mini diagrams, portrait tokens on a pitch that fits the viewport, a spotlight panel with the best bench options for the selected role, bench cards (tap or drag onto the pitch), tactic presets.

## Version 3 (match engine visuals, looks, menu, postseason)
- `ui_stage.js` replaces the old Stage. Ball rule: the ball is always at a player's feet, in a player's hands, in flight from a player's kick/throw/header, rolling out after a missed shot, set down on a spot by the player who carried it there, or in the net. Flights home onto the receiver's live foot/head/hands, so passes always arrive. Before every goal the scorer owns the ball and strikes it himself; set pieces are fetched, carried and placed by the taker; keepers fetch from the net and restart play.
- Sync: the engine resolves a minute first, but the live screen shows nothing from it until the Stage calls `emit(e)` at the visual moment (goal = ball over the line; save = ball in the keeper's hands; cards = referee shows the card; shootout kicks as they land). Leftover events (period markers) flush in order after the minute; stats, possession and momentum update after the minute. Skip/pause/resume are race-safe (`L.skipping`, loop restart in `finally`).
- Audits: `test/sync.js` (full match in Chromium; checks loose-ball frames, scorer = shooter, goal announced only over the line, saves in hands, commentary only via emit) and `test/shootout.js`. Typical result: 0 bad launches, 0 early commentary, 0 air shots, every goal by its scorer; the only ownerless time is a missed shot rolling out before the keeper collects it.
- `looks.js`: deterministic appearance per player (12 skin tones, 10 hair colors, 15 hairstyles, 5 face shapes, eyes, brows, nose, expression, facial hair, ears, freckles, build, sleeves, boots, headband, tape). Edited looks are stored on `p.look`; Appearance tab in the profile with live portrait and animated in-game figure. Portraits on player cards, lineups, goal banner.
- Traits start at 0 for everyone and are edit-only (0–10). Weights in `TRAIT` (engine.js) make them the strongest levers: striker goals/game 0.27 at 0 → 0.72 at 5 → 1.19 at 10, versus 0.62 for +15 shooting.
- Odds: moneyline only, proportional vig (6% three-way, 4.5% advancement), US ladder rounding, "EVEN" near 50%.
- Menu: persistent canvas showcase match behind the title (skipped with reduced motion). Postseason: "Road to State" journey with trophy checkpoints, seeding race before sectionals, bracket-is-set reveal, matchday title card before kickoff, trophy case on the School page.
- Fixed: `.live` root rule collided with the nav "LIVE" badge (now `#live-root>.live`).

## Architecture
- `data.js` – name/town/mascot vocabularies, stadium tiers.
- `engine.js` – pure domain logic, no DOM: generation, development, lineups, seeded minute-by-minute match engine, odds, competitions, awards, records, rollover, migrations.
- `ui_core.js` – IndexedDB persistence, theme engine with contrast protection, procedural Web Audio, crest and stadium SVG.
- `ui_views.js`, `ui_match.js`, `ui_main.js` – views, match night, controller.

## Persistence
- IndexedDB `careers` (compact current state per slot), `logs` (one record per fixture, indexed by slot+season, loaded lazily per season), `meta` (slot cards). localStorage holds only global prefs (theme, sound, panels).
- Career settings (difficulty, cards, suspensions, Manager Mode, legacy rule) live inside each save.
- A match in progress is saved at halftime and when paused (`C.live`) and resumes from its stored RNG state.
- Backup = JSON copy/paste (downloads are blocked inside the artifact viewer). Deployment, accounts and server sync (Cloudflare D1 / Supabase auth in the original) are **not** part of this build.

## Match engine
- One seeded results stream; a separate cosmetic stream for defensive stats, assists, touches and replay paths. Surface and stadium choices never enter the engine (tested).
- Odds come from 240 fast runs of the same engine with independent seeds. League previews are a regulation 3-way market; knockouts show advancement odds including ET and penalties.
- Calibration (6 worlds, 5,040 matches): predicted favourite win 45/54/66/74/84/94% → observed 44/52/71/74/82/95% (±3 pts 95% CI per bucket).

## Legacy "Arcade favorites" rule (off by default)
Applies only to the user's team when the realistic model gives it ≥85% (level 1) or ≥90.5% (level 2). Displayed odds always come from the realistic model, so there is no odds→boost→odds loop. Measured: base 86% (-920) → 96% wins, 5.0 goals; base 91% (-1940) → 99%, 7.8 goals; base 94% → 100%, 8.7 goals.

## Design decisions
- One award per player per season; All-League counts. Precedence: MVP, Goal Scorer, Playmaker, DPOY, GK, Tournament MVP, Breakout, Freshman, All-League 1st, 2nd. Championships are team honors. Awards finalize once after the State final whether or not the user is still alive; `migrate()` back-fills missing awards idempotently.
- Single-season records = Varsity regular season only. Career = Varsity regular + postseason. Single-game = Varsity league + postseason (labelled). Preseason never counts.
- Varsity starters for a match are a repaired copy; saved lineups are never silently rewritten.
- Manager Mode direction pre-assigns goal minutes and runs the normal engine, so stats, standings, records and awards follow the same pipeline. Manual entry sets goal totals (and a shootout winner for tied knockouts); individual scorers are generated, not hand-picked.

## Verified (headless Node + Chromium)
Distinct worlds per slot; 168 regular fixtures with no duplicates and reconciled standings; every formation fills 1 GK + 10 outfield; promotion cascades keep depth and GK coverage; halftime pauses at 40, Proceed is idempotent, opponents get no talk; directed winners always win; shootout kicks never count as goals; five-season run with rollovers, awards unique every year; reload restores career and match archive; no horizontal scroll at 390px.
